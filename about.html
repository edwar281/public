About

Hi, I'm Will.

I build analytics and data science teams that ship, and I use AI to make them faster.

Today I'm Director of Analytics, Data Science and AI at Spectrum, where my team of 13 supports about 70 product managers across Internet, WiFi and Voice. I'm also an AI ambassador within our product organization, helping bring generative and agentic AI into day-to-day work.

Before Spectrum I spent nearly six years at Charles Schwab, first building enterprise analytics for Digital Product and the Call Center, then directing AI and data science through the TD Ameritrade merger. Earlier I led operations analytics at Discover and ecommerce analytics at Sears.

I started out in education research, and a PhD with a statistics cognate still shapes how I work: careful measurement, honest uncertainty, and results people can act on. My strongest skill is translation between data scientists, engineers and executives.
